The fine print

Redact runs entirely in your browser. Because it never sends your information anywhere, there's nothing on a server to leak. Removal forms and emails open in your own apps — you stay in control of every request.

Broker opt-out pages and addresses change often, and some brokers re-add you over time. Check back and re-run removals every few months. This tool isn't legal advice.
