    How your data is handled
    On device

    Everything you type is saved only in this browser. Close the tab and it's still here; clear it and it's gone. There's no account and no database.

    On scan

    When you press Scan or Clean, your details are sent to Redact's scanning engine for that job only — used to find and remove your listings, then discarded. It isn't sold, shared, or kept.

    Open

    Redact is open source. Anyone can read exactly what it does. View the code on GitHub · Read the plain-English writeup.

    The fine print

    Broker opt-out pages and addresses change often, and some brokers re-add you over time. If your listings come back later, run Redact again — there's no schedule and nothing runs in the background.

    Redact helps you exercise privacy rights you already have. It isn't legal advice.

    California residents: the state's DROP platform can delete you from every registered broker with one request.
